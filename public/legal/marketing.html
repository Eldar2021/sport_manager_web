The problem

Billiards, table tennis and chess halls still track table time on paper. The result: calculation errors and arguments with customers, managers "forgetting" to write down hours, and owners with no idea how their business really performs.

The solution

DuLa runs on the manager's phone, calculates table time automatically, and gives the owner real-time revenue and usage reports.
