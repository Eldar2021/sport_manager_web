Frequently asked questions

How do managers sign up?

A manager needs an invite code from the owner. The owner generates it in the app (Profile → Managers), shares the code, and the manager enters it during registration.

I forgot my password

Use “Forgot password” on the login screen. We email a temporary password to your registered address. Sign in with it, then change your password in the app.

How do I switch venues?

Tap the venue name in the top bar of the Home screen and pick another venue from the list.

How much does it cost?

1000 som per venue per month, with a 14-day free trial for new owners.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → scroll down → “Delete account” → “Delete forever”. Full details on the Account Deletion page.
